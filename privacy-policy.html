Privacy Policy

Living Manna — a bilingual offline Bible reading app

Effective date: 7 May 2026

Plain-English summary. Living Manna does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app runs entirely on your device. There are no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs that report usage, and no servers we operate that receive information about you.

1. Who we are

Living Manna ("the app") is developed and maintained by an independent developer ("we", "us"). The app is distributed through Google Play under the package name com.livingmanna.livingmanna. You can contact us at tesfayegirma360@gmail.com for any privacy question.

2. Information we do not collect

We want to be specific because "we don't collect anything" is easy to claim and hard to verify. The app contains no code that performs any of the following:

  • No account creation or login. The app does not have user accounts.
  • No analytics SDKs (no Firebase Analytics, no Crashlytics, no Mixpanel, no Amplitude, no Sentry, no telemetry of any kind).
  • No advertising SDKs and no advertising identifiers (no AdMob, no Meta Audience Network).
  • No location collection. The app never requests location permission.
  • No contacts, calendar, microphone, camera, SMS, call log, health, or biometric access.
  • No device identifiers (no Android ID, IMEI, MAC, or advertising ID is read or transmitted).
  • No crash reporting service. If the app crashes, the report stays with the operating system; we never see it.
  • No background network activity initiated by the app.

3. Information stored on your device

To make the app work, the following information is stored locally on your device using Android's standard preferences storage. None of it leaves your device unless you explicitly export it (see "Export and sharing" below).

  • Reading progress (last book/chapter/verse you opened).
  • Bookmarks, highlights, and personal notes you create on verses.
  • Reading history and reading-plan progress.
  • Reading-time statistics.
  • Your interface preferences: language, theme, font size, haptics, and reminder time.
  • Your enrolled reading plans.

You can clear all of this at any time by uninstalling the app, or by clearing the app's data from your device's Settings → Apps → Living Manna → Storage.

4. Permissions the app requests

Each Android permission the app asks for is used only for the feature it is named for. Most are optional — declining them disables only that feature, not the rest of the app.

Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, on Android 13+)

Used only to deliver the daily verse reminder and reading-plan reminders that you opt into. Notifications are generated locally on your device. There is no push channel and no remote messaging.

Schedule exact alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, on Android 12+)

Used to fire your daily reminder at the exact minute you choose, even when the device is in battery-saver or Doze mode. If you do not grant this permission, reminders still fire but may be delayed by the operating system.

Run on device boot (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED)

Used to re-register your scheduled reminders after the device restarts so you do not miss them. The app does nothing else at boot.

Wake lock (WAKE_LOCK)

Used briefly when a scheduled reminder fires so the device is awake long enough to display the notification.

Photos / media (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES on Android 13+; legacy READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on older versions)

Used only when you choose a photo to use as a verse-card background, or when you save a verse-card image to your gallery. The app reads the file you pick and writes the file you save; it does not browse, index, or upload any other files.

5. Export and sharing — initiated by you

Two features in the app produce data that may leave your device, and both are triggered explicitly by you:

  • Backup & restore. Settings → Backup writes a JSON file containing your bookmarks, notes, history, plans, and preferences, then opens the system share sheet. You choose where it goes (Drive, Files, email, etc.). The app sends nothing automatically and never to a destination we control.
  • Verse sharing. Tapping share on a verse opens the system share sheet (or a deep link to Telegram, WhatsApp, or Facebook share dialogs). The verse text and reference are passed to the destination of your choice. We do not receive a copy.

Once you hand data to another app or service via the share sheet, that app's privacy policy governs what happens next.

6. Children

The app is appropriate for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, regardless of age. There is no account and no profile to create.

7. Bible translation copyrights

The app includes Bible translations whose text is the property of their respective rights holders. Each translation's copyright notice is shown inside the app under Settings → About → Translations. Distribution rights for licensed translations are obtained from the publishers; the app does not modify the translation text.

8. Data deletion

Because the app stores everything locally and does not have user accounts, the simplest way to delete all data is to uninstall the app or clear its storage from your device's Settings. Since we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on a server.

If you have any further deletion request — for example, regarding a backup file you previously emailed us — write to tesfayegirma360@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

9. Security

App data is stored using Android's standard application sandbox, which isolates it from other apps. Backup files you export are written in plain JSON because portability and inspectability matter for a backup; treat the exported file as you would any other personal file when choosing where to send it.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy — for example, if a future version adds an opt-in feature that involves any kind of network call — this page will be updated and the effective date at the top will change. Material changes will also be highlighted inside the app.
