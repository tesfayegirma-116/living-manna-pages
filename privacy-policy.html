2. Information We Do Not Collect

We believe that true privacy begins with data minimization. Living Manna is engineered without any code paths or mechanisms to collect or transmit your personal information. Specifically:

  • ✕
    No User Accounts or Authentication: You do not need to sign up, sign in, or provide an email, username, phone number, or password.
  • ✕
    No Analytics or Telemetry SDKs: We do not include Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, Sentry, Segment, or any custom telemetry tracking code.
  • ✕
    No Advertising SDKs: We do not display advertisements, embed Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network, or any programmatic ad frameworks.
  • ✕
    No Tracking Identifiers: The app never requests Apple's Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), Google Advertising ID (GAID), Android ID, IMEI, or hardware MAC addresses.
  • ✕
    No Location or Biometric Data: The app never accesses GPS, cellular geolocation, Wi-Fi SSIDs, Apple Touch ID / Face ID data, or biometric sensors.
  • ✕
    No Background Network Activity: The app operates offline and does not connect to remote servers in the background to log your Bible reading activity or app usage.

3. Information Stored On Your Device

To deliver a rich, personalized Bible study experience, Living Manna stores data locally inside the secure sandbox environment provided by your operating system (iOS Sandboxed Storage and Android App Sandboxed Storage).

This strictly local data includes:

  • ✓
    Reading Progress & History: The last book, chapter, and verse you viewed, plus recent reading history for rapid resume.
  • ✓
    Annotations & Personal Notes: Verse bookmarks, highlights (with custom color tags), and personal study notes created by you.
  • ✓
    Reading Plans & Streaks: Enrolled Scripture reading plans, completed milestones, and calculated streak counts.
  • ✓
    Reading Statistics: Aggregated on-device reading metrics (e.g., minutes spent reading, chapters completed).
  • ✓
    User Preferences: Interface language, theme selection (System / Light / Dark), typography size, line spacing, parallel reading layout, and reminder times.
100% User Control: You can completely erase all locally stored data at any time by uninstalling the app, or via your operating system settings (e.g., Android Settings → Apps → Living Manna → Clear Data, or iOS Settings → iPhone Storage → Delete App).

4. Device Permissions & Hardware Access

When Living Manna requests device permissions, each permission is strictly dedicated to enabling a specific user-requested feature. All processing occurs locally:

  • 🔔
    Notifications (iOS UserNotifications & Android POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used exclusively to deliver opt-in daily verse reminders and reading plan alerts. All notification scheduling is handled locally on the device OS scheduler. There is no remote push notification server.
  • ⏰
    Exact Alarms & Boot Receiver (Android SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM & RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Allows your chosen reminder times to fire punctually even in low-power or Doze modes, and re-registers your local schedule upon device restart.
  • 🖼️
    Photo Library & Media Storage (iOS Photo Access & Android READ_MEDIA_IMAGES): Used only when you explicitly create and save a verse-card image to your photo gallery, or choose a custom background wallpaper image from your library. The app accesses only the specific photo selected.
  • ⚡
    Wake Lock (Android WAKE_LOCK): Briefly ensures the CPU is active during the exact moment a local alarm triggers so the notification can be generated cleanly.

5. Data Export & User-Initiated Sharing

Your data belongs to you. Living Manna provides manual export and sharing capabilities that are strictly triggered by your deliberate actions:

  • 💾
    Backup & Restore (JSON Export): Under Settings → Backup, you can generate an unencrypted, human-readable JSON backup containing your bookmarks, notes, reading history, and preferences. The app triggers the native OS Share Sheet (AirDrop, Files, Google Drive, email) so you can store it wherever you choose.
  • 📤
    Verse & Card Sharing: When you choose to share a verse or generated wallpaper image, the app passes the content directly to the native system share dialog (or deep link to WhatsApp, Telegram, etc.). We never receive a copy or record of what you shared or whom you shared it with.

Please note: Once data is shared outside of Living Manna via the OS Share Sheet, the privacy practices of the recipient service or application will govern that data.

Living Manna is safe for users of all ages, including children. Because the application does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in the United States and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR-K) in the European Union.

8. Data Retention, Deletion & Security

Data Retention: Because we maintain no external servers, databases, or user telemetry, we hold zero records of your data. Your data exists only as long as you keep the app installed on your device or retain an exported JSON backup file.

Data Deletion: You have absolute authority over your data. To permanently delete all notes, bookmarks, reading progress, and settings:

  • 📱
    On iOS: Delete Living Manna from your Home Screen or App Library. iOS will immediately remove the app sandbox and all contained files.
  • 🤖
    On Android: Navigate to Settings → Apps → Living Manna → Storage → Clear Storage / Clear Data, or uninstall the application.

Security: All local databases and preferences are protected by iOS and Android application sandbox barriers, preventing other unauthorized apps from accessing your Living Manna study records.
