Common questions

Does the app work offline?
Yes. Every included translation is bundled with the app, so reading, search, notes and highlights all work with no connection.
Which translations are included?
King James Version, Amharic 1954, the Easy-to-Read Version, and the Amharic New Amharic Standard Version. Availability can vary by region according to each publisher's terms.
Where are my notes, highlights and bookmarks stored?
On your device only. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no account to create. You can export a backup from Settings if you want a copy.
How do I move my data to a new phone?
In Settings, export a backup on the old device, transfer the file, then import it on the new one.
The home screen widget isn't updating.
Open the app once so it can refresh the day's verse, then remove and re-add the widget. iOS decides how often widgets may refresh, so a short delay is normal.
How do I report a mistake in the text?
Please email the book, chapter and verse along with the translation name, and it will be checked against the source.
